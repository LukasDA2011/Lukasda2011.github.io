Black Box Project

The "Black Box Project" was a project where a group had to work together and create a visual and virtual representation of what we thought was inside "The Mystery Black Box."

Claim: The Black Box has a water wheel inside of the box that would transport the water poured in at different amounts each time.

We made our water wheel by using multiple pieces of cardboard with cups and made wheel spin by using Plastic straws and skewers. Then, we put a bucket with a tube below to collect the water and put a tube at the end so we can take the water into a measurement tube.
